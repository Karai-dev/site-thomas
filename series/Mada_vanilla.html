Towards Sustainable Vanilla

Madagascar

Year
2022
Photographs
20
Category
Commissions · Institutions

World leading producer of vanilla, one the most expensive spices in the world, Madagascar is facing acute climate change, threatening the precious plant. In parts of the country, efforts are being made to create sustainable practices that impact both vanilla and its neighboring biodiversity, which is one of the richest on the planet.

A reportage for Prova.
