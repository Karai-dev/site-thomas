Agricultural Development in Tanzania

Tanzania

Year
2021
Photographs
21
Category
Commissions · Institutions

Commissioned by Imageo for the African Development Bank (AfDB), this photo series highlights how expanded access to quality fertilizers and modern soil inputs is transforming rural livelihoods across Tanzania.

By empowering smallholder farmers to increase crop yields, build resilience against climate shifts, and strengthen local food security, the initiative illustrates the pivotal role of targeted agricultural investment in driving regional economic development.

Commissioned by Check-in Films for AfDB.
