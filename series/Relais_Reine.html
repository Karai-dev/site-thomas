Le Relais de la Reine

Madagascar

Year

Elegantly integrated into the granite cliffs and flora of Isalo, Le Relais de la Reine blends architectural heritage with the serene luxury of Madagascar's high plateaus.

This series captures the effortless lifestyle within its stone-hewn walls—from the warm, quiet comfort of its rooms to its panoramic pool and serene facilities—offering an intimate refuge carved directly into the ancient wild.
