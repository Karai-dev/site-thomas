Alefa

Madagascar

Year

Navigating the untouched waters of Northwestern Madagascar, Alefa preserves the ancient art of boutre (dhow) sailing to offer slow, immersational journeys along remote coastlines.

Guided by traditional seamanship and the natural rhythm of the tides, these traditional wooden vessels connect travelers with secluded islands, coastal communities, and the untamed maritime heritage of the Mozambique Channel.
