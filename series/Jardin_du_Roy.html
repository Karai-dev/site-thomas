Madagascar

Seamlessly carved into the rugged sandstone landscape of Isalo, Le Jardin du Roy offers a refined retreat where raw nature meets understated comfort.

This lifestyle series captures the harmonious dialogue between the lodge’s indigenous stonework architecture, the tranquil comfort of its sunlit rooms, and the leisurely rhythms of its oasis-like amenities—framing an immersive living experience in the heart of Madagascar’s wild southwest.
