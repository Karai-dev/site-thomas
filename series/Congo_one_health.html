One Health Approach in South Kivu

South Kivu, DRC

Year
2023
Photographs
23
Category
Commissions · NGOs

The South Kivu, and especially the Kahuzi Biega National Park, is a hotbed for biodiversity. The proximity of wildlife, among which the eastern lowland gorilla, human settlments, and their cattle, increases the risk for a zoonotic disease to break loose. VSF-Belgium, together with MDM and their local partners, work with local communities within their One Health approach: improving human health, together with animal and environment health for reduced risks.
