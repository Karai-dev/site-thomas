The White Village

Madagascar

Year
2025
Photographs
9
Category
Documentary

Around a bend in a winding road in central Madagascar lies the small village of Soatanana, nicknamed the 'White Village.'

Dressed in white year-round, the followers of the Lord’s Revival live there as a community, guided by their beliefs. They gather at church every Sunday, and the melodious hymns of their long service ring out. A place untouched by time.
